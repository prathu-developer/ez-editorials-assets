🧠 Global Intelligence (Elo)

While your Weekly Cup score resets every Sunday, your Elo Rating is a permanent, lifetime measure of your consistency and intelligence. It increases when you answer difficult questions correctly, and bleeds (decreases) if your accuracy drops week-on-week. Keep grinding to unlock higher academic titles!
