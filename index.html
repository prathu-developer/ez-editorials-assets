📜 Guide: Quiz Cup & Elo

⚙️ Dynamic Scoring System

Points adjust based on global class accuracy. The harder the question is for the class, the higher the reward and the lower the penalty!

Tier Correct Wrong
T1 (Very Easy) +1.0 -0.42
T2 (Easy) +2.0 -0.60

T5 (Boss) +5.0 -0.42

🧠 Global Intelligence (Elo)

While your Weekly Cup score resets every Sunday, your Elo Rating is a permanent, lifetime measure of your consistency and intelligence. It increases when you answer difficult questions correctly, and bleeds (decreases) if your accuracy drops week-on-week. Keep grinding to unlock higher academic titles!

🏅 The League Journey

Compete, climb the leaderboard, and unlock higher leagues. There are no speed advantages—only accuracy and consistency matter.
